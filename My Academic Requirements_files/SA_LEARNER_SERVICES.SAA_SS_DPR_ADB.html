 |-- ABOUT THE ACADEMIC REQUIREMENTS REPORT --| 
Group Box

This Academic Requirements Report (ARR) displays groups of course requirements that are unique to you, depending on your year of admission or re-admission to a program in the university, the declare date of each major or minor, and any exceptions entered on your individual student record. This report is to assist you in course planning; it does not represent an irrevocable contract between the student and the university. Final confirmation of degree requirements is subject to department, school, and university approval.

In addition to course requirements, undergraduate students must satisfy credit-hour requirements for graduation and residency. Students must complete at least 120 credit hours to graduate. To satisfy the Required Hours in Residence Needed for Graduation, students must take their final, uninterrupted 45 credit hours of instruction or a minimum of 60 credit hours in residence at Loyola.

Students should consistently review this report with their academic advisor and department/school advisors each academic term to ensure that all academic requirements are being met. Academic advisors will report any adjustments to the appropriate LOCUS staff to be reflected on the ARR.

** When declaring a Major or Minor, all students are required to complete current requirements in effect during the term when the major is officially declared
